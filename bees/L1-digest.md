## The rules that make this safe
1. Everything reads the feed. Nothing re-researches. A bullet may contain only what is in the editions: a story's `headline`, `dek` and `tickers`, the values of its claim rows at status `ok` or `sec`, and (PRE-OPEN only, for "Due" items) the edition's `catalysts` list. No web search, no price fetch, no memory, no rounding that changes a figure.
2. Facts only. A bullet states what happened and the number. Never a view, a forecast, a "why it matters", a "should", "cheap/expensive/undervalued", an exclamation, an emoji, a hashtag. Describe a move only as the claim row does. Never tie a price move to a cause with "after", "on", "as", "because" or "due to" unless the story's dek or a claim row states that link; otherwise give the move alone, or the event alone.
3. Verified only. Use only stories with `state` VERIFIED and `level` ok, not withdrawn or corrected away by a later edition's `correction` object or CORRECTION story. If a later edition corrects a figure, use the corrected one.
4. Fresh only. Intraday and pre-market prices: at most 2 hours old at NOW (from the claim row's `asof`); older → leave that price out. Company events, filings and official data: within 24 hours; anything before today names the day ("after Tuesday's close", "on 13 Oct"). Never "today", "tonight", "this morning" or "yesterday" in the text — the header carries the date.
5. Two approvals or no send. The post goes out only after Colette (copy) and then Lisa (marketing/FCA) both approve. Standing authorisation: Alex, 2 and 5 Oct 2026 for automatic wire posts after both reviewers approve ("it goes automatically right?", "why not automatic?"), and 14 Oct 2026 for this twice-a-day digest in its place ("ok agree get it all applied now"). That is the user confirmation for the one publish this run makes.
6. One post per run. Never more. Never touch anything else: never edit, schedule, publish or delete a draft this run did not create; never write to `desk`, `editions`, `notes`, `os` or `journalism/config`; never touch any scheduled task.

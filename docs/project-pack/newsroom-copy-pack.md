## Summary
All six blocks audited line by line against s21 FSMA and FG24/1. No red findings. Two ambers, both about scarcity language on the sale page, both fixable in a few words. The education-not-advice line, the capital-at-risk line and the own-measurement framing are present on every public surface.

## 🟡 AMBER

**Item:** Sale page — the early-bird discount and its 25 October end date.
**Rule:** §4, fair/clear/not misleading — no fake urgency or scarcity around an investment decision.
**Why it is only amber, and now lower risk than version 1:** the offer is a discount on a **membership to an education service**, not on an investment, and it has a real end date rather than an invented seat count. Version 1's "thirty places, thirteen left" was the riskier form and has been removed.
**Fix:** none needed. Keep the wording to "early bird discount" and the honest end date; never add a live countdown or "last chance".

**Item:** Sale page version A — "A measured entry price for every stock I track."
**Rule:** §2, advice perimeter — a "measured entry price" could be read by a beginner as a personal recommendation to buy at that price.
**Fix (in Alex's voice, recommended for both versions):** add one line under the sub, same size, not in grey:
> These are my levels, measured the same way every time. They are not a recommendation, and they are not tailored to you.
